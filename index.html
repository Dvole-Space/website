Dvole Space is a research lab/group whose primary focus is on foundational sciences. With the AI revolution, the core research spaces, especially those of mathematics, theoretical physics and computer science, are flooded with results. Loads of information, a lot of it, very useful and insightful, but a lot of clutter as well.

This very phenomenon should direct us to focus on even more foundational stuff. Human society's collective knowledge has been constantly directed towards a more fundamental/metaphysical model of the universe. With AI, intelligently brute-forcing proofs for all these mathematical conjectures, and even the empirical AI research, which is reaching a saturation point, taking a step further towards the metaphysical dimension, will really shape the new research landscapes.

Further, one of the core philosophies behind the research I intend to do is to see the big picture first and then optimise the smaller ones accordingly. In other words, I am highly inclined to apply insights and instincts from all these fields—maths, physics, philosophy, etc, to each other, and not just perform research only for the sake of a particular field.

The website is going to grow actively, and expect major changes along the way, as it will first start with somewhat raw materials, and polish and shape itself as the research grows. I doubt many will be interested in joining it. Still, either way, I am not really looking for any collaborators yet, I have to work a bit myself to make sure that first, I clearly, at least to some extent, understand the core of what I am envisioning, and with that, expect research notes, suggested readings, blogs and eventually some research bits in the coming months.
