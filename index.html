Dvole

An independent research initiative concerned with foundational questions in mathematics, physics, epistemology, computation, and scientific practice.

What does it actually mean for us to understand something?

Different theories can describe related phenomena, different representations can preserve the same structure, and different observations can justify very different conclusions. We are interested in the questions that emerge from those relationships.
